Frequently asked questions

What makes a perfume luxury?

Usually the quality and rarity of ingredients, the richness of the composition and the care in the bottle and packaging.

Are luxury perfumes worth the price?

That depends on what you value. Richer ingredients and higher concentration often mean more depth and longer wear.

How do I choose a luxury fragrance?

Pick a note family you enjoy, such as oud, rose or leather, then use the consultation form and we will narrow it down.

Can I order a luxury perfume as a gift?

Yes. Gift packaging and a handwritten-style note card are available at checkout.

How should I store a fine fragrance?

Keep it in its box, away from heat, sunlight and humidity, so the notes stay true.
